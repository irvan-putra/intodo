Real and Demo feeds can remain online together. Configure their two HTTPS feed addresses once in the drawer, then use Demo / Back to real feed. Setup preserves the existing pairing key and asks which role the previously saved feed had. Real and demo caches, freshness and sync status are separate, with a recovery copy of the original cache.

Entering Demo clears visible app rows and widget snapshots before selecting synthetic items. Demo has a persistent banner, hides bot and review-canvas shortcuts, and refuses feeds without the synthetic section/item markers. These markers catch an accidental wrong feed selection; they are not content classification. Returning to real items requires confirmation. Stale in-flight sync responses are discarded after switching modes or changing addresses.
