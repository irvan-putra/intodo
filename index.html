Review each change from your phone

Interactive design prototype

The best ideas from crev, Unison, Haskell and Lean, merged into one review surface for the intodo project. Cards live on a canvas, not in pages or an endless feed: they stay where you put them, and the layout persists on this device and browser (localStorage).

The idea

Every change becomes a card you can read in under a minute: what changed, which exact code it is, who reviewed it, and what was tested or proved. Cards connect spatially to their evidence, and what has not been checked stays visible in the same size type as what has.

The example below uses the real v0.4 narrow-layout change. "Real" chips mark grounded facts; "mock" chips mark illustrative entries. This is a working interactive mockup, not a production review system - nothing is adopted or built into the app.

The review canvas

Try it

Drag the background to pan, drag a card to move it, pinch or use + and - to zoom, tap a card's Focus to read it. The canvas is bounded, not infinite.
